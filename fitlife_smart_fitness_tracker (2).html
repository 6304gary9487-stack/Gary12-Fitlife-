IMPROVE HEALTH AWARENESS

Health Tips — Exercise, Nutrition & Recovery

Practical, beginner-friendly guidance to support safe movement and everyday well-being.

🏃‍♂️
01 · MOVEMENT

Exercise Basics

  • ✓ Start with 150 min/week: Break it into manageable 30-minute brisk walks or light jogs 5 days a week.
  • ✓ Mix Cardio & Strength: Combine aerobic activities (running, cycling) with 2 days of strength training.
  • ✓ Break up sitting time: Stand up and stretch for 3–5 minutes every hour of desk work or study.
Consistency matters more than intensity.
🥗
02 · FUEL

Nutrition Fundamentals

  • ✓ Stay Hydrated: Drink water before, during, and after workouts to maintain energy and focus.
  • ✓ Balanced Plates: Pair complex carbohydrates (whole grains, fruit) with lean protein for muscle repair.
  • ✓ Mindful Snacking: Choose nuts, yogurt, or fruit over sugary drinks for steady daytime energy.
Fuel your body for sustainable daily energy.
🧘‍♀️
03 · RESTORATION

Recovery & Mobility

  • ✓ Schedule Rest Days: Muscles grow and adapt during recovery—plan at least 1–2 rest days weekly.
  • ✓ Warm-up & Cool-down: Spend 5 minutes stretching tight muscles after exercise to reduce soreness.
  • ✓ Prioritize 7–9 Hours of Sleep: Quality sleep regulates mood, metabolism, and physical recovery.
Rest is an active part of your fitness plan.

01 · THE CHALLENGE

Why Do We Need FitLife?

01 · Sedentary Routines

Study, desk work, and screen time cause people to sit for long periods without realizing it.

02 · Low Tracking Awareness

Without simple personal records, it is hard to see how active we really are each week.

03 · Motivation Gaps

Many people start exercising enthusiastically but lose motivation when progress feels unclear.
